# EcoSort AI - Quick Start Guide

## Welcome to EcoSort AI!

Your AI-powered waste classification and sustainability platform is ready to use. Here's how to get started with all the amazing AI features.

## Getting Started (5 Minutes)

### 1. First Classification
1. Navigate to **Classify** page
2. Upload an image or take a photo
3. Wait for AI analysis (1-2 seconds)
4. View detailed classification with:
   - Waste category
   - Confidence score
   - Disposal methods
   - Environmental impact
   - Material analysis

### 2. View Your Dashboard
1. Go to **Dashboard**
2. See your environmental impact:
   - Carbon saved
   - Items classified
   - Recycling stats
3. Check your goals and progress
4. View your leaderboard ranking

### 3. Explore AI Hub
1. Click **AI Hub** in navigation
2. Discover 6 AI-powered features
3. Get personalized recommendations
